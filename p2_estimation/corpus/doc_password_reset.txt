Resetting Your Corporate Password

Passwords must be reset every 90 days per company security policy.
To reset your password, navigate to the self-service portal and
select "Reset Password" from the account menu.

You will need to verify your identity using the one-time passcode
sent to your registered device, the same mechanism used for VPN
authentication. After verification, choose a new password that meets
the complexity requirements: minimum 12 characters, at least one
number, and one special character.

Password resets take effect immediately across all connected systems,
including VPN access and email.
