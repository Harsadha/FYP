Setting Up Company VPN Access

To connect to the corporate network remotely, employees must install
the approved VPN client and authenticate with their single sign-on
credentials. The VPN client can be downloaded from the internal
software portal.

Once installed, open the client and enter the gateway address
vpn.company.internal. You will be prompted for your username and a
one-time passcode sent to your registered device. Sessions expire
after 12 hours of inactivity and must be re-authenticated.

If you experience connection drops, check that split-tunneling is
disabled in the client settings, as this is the most common cause of
intermittent failures reported by the helpdesk.
