Paid Time Off Policy

Full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at a rate based on
tenure, starting at 15 days per year and increasing to 25 days after
five years of service.

PTO requests are submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks
in advance for requests longer than three consecutive days. Unused
PTO carries over up to a maximum of 10 days into the following year;
any balance above that is forfeited.
